# 00411c80 — inferred_Display_DrawText
Status: understood · Cluster: 31 · Convention: thiscall(Display *this, byte *text, uint colour, int bank, int centreX, int y, int maxWidth, uint16 tag, char centred, int lineHeight) — RET 0x24
**Behaviour:** lays out text for the frame and appends text records (0x70 bytes each, array at display +0x680, count at +0x13a0): the glyph widths come from the font bank's sequence records (character c uses record c - 0x21, advance = record +0x10 + 2; an unknown character or space advances by the record at +0xd48 + 2 and is stored as 0x7f), the line breaks at the last space before the width is exceeded, each line is stored with its own x (centred on centreX; kept inside the 0x280-wide screen with a margin of one space width) and the lines go down by lineHeight (0 = 1.5 times the font's height at +0xd4a), vertically centred on y when `centred`, and kept on screen (0x1e0 high). The record carries colour (565, halved in colour mode when +0x67c is set), the bank and the tag (+0x668). The records are drawn by the compose step. Characters are single bytes in the DOS code page. **Layout rules found by comparing:** the maximum width is cut to (320 - space advance) * 2 and the centre becomes 320 when it is larger; a line ends when the next glyph passes the width or at the end of the text; if it contains a space or an unknown character it is broken at the nearest space going back (dropped), otherwise after the character that passed the width; without `centred` the lines are left aligned in a box of `maxWidth` centred on x (this is what the choice menu does: its text starts at the left margin, it is not centred); y is clamped to [line height, bottom]. The call also sets the display's +0x668 (the layer depth used by compose) to `tag`. The record has room for 100 characters and the array for 30 records (the counter follows it): longer lines or more records overrun.
**Port:** `TextQueue::add` (core/textqueue.cpp), used through `ScreenDisplay::drawText`. **Validation:** gen_vectors_text.py + drivers/text_driver.cpp: 400 cases, 3,433 laid-out lines (x, y, colour, font, characters) and 400 layer depths identical, plus boundary cases for the margins; 22 of 25 mutations caught (three are equivalent: equality at the clamps gives the same value). Not covered: lines over 100 characters, more than 29 lines, control characters, a back-up past the start of the line. Deviation: unbounded lines and records.
