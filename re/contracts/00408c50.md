# 00408c50 — inferred_Level_ResolveOverlap
Status: emu-compared (rectangle and shaped paths) · Cluster: 13 · Convention: thiscall(Level *this, Obj *hitter, Obj *other) — RET 8

**Purpose (confirmed):** Decides whether the hitter touches the other object during this frame's movement and, if so, queues a touch.
**Behaviour (rectangle path):** the hitter's movement relative to the other: dx = (A.x2 − A.x1) − (B.x2 − B.x1), dy = (A.y2 − B.y2) − A.y1 + B.y1 over the moved areas (+0x20..+0x2c); A's hit rectangle (+0x10..+0x1c) is shifted to where it was when B was drawn (by A.moved.x1 − B.moved.x1, A.moved.y1 − B.moved.y1) and swept by (dx, dy) against B's hit rectangle (00409280). A result below 0 ends the call. The shape entry counts of both current sequences (u16 at record +0x1c) are read: if either is 0 the sweep result is the answer.
**Shaped path (read, not ported):** when both objects have shape entries the sweep result is refined: if one has one entry and the other more, a point test (00409560) is made at the start and at the end of the movement; if both have 2 or more, the movement is walked in steps of one pixel along its longer axis from the sweep's fraction, building the other's outline in the hitter's frame (00409c10) and testing the two outlines against each other (00409790) in both directions, the answer becoming the step reached, or −1 for no touch. Port: `Level::shapedContactTime` (core/level.cpp) follows the read path; one entry vs several: the point test gives 0 or −1 (no movement: −1 when the hitter has one entry, 1 otherwise, as read); stepped walk gives (steps − left − 1)/steps as a 16.16 time or −1. Compared with the original through the whole collision pass (gen_vectors_collide.py now gives sequences of types 2 and 4 with convex outlines, single-point outlines and zero movement): 3,496 passes (3,709 touches), 8,816 state checks identical; 676 of 2,500 cases dropped because the original read outside an outline or ran away. Mutations caught: shaped path off, step rounding, final time formula, both-ways overlap test, point-test inversions, the movement-free time of 1; one equivalent mutant (sign of dy at 0) survives. Not covered: non-convex outlines.
**The touch:** depth = the larger of the two sort values (u16), or 100 if either is the player (global 004420fc). The touch (hitter, other, time, depth) is inserted into the level's touch list (+0x78) before the first touch whose time is larger, or equal with a larger signed depth, else at the end.
**Port:** Level::resolveOverlap.
**Validation:** see 00408890.
**Open questions:** whether shipped outlines are convex; the shipped objects with shape entries (types 2 and 4 sequences) decide how much of the shaped path matters.
