**Behaviour (read, not ported):** not walking (+0xd5 = 0): the scale (+0xfc) := the walk area's perspective scale at his position (0x414740, 0x100 when there is no walk area), then the speaker update 0x4101f0 (see Speaker). Walking: the vector to the destination (dx, dy); the animation is advanced (0x401860) and the movement its current frame adds since the last frame (fields +0x28/+0x2a of the player against +0xe8/+0xec) is multiplied by the scale (0x100 = 1:1); the object's own tick (slot 2) runs; for left/right facing (3, 4) the vertical step is corrected by (stepX * dy) / dx, for up/down (1, 2) the horizontal step by (stepY * dx) / dy, so he follows the straight line to the destination at the speed of the animation; the 24.8 position (+0xe0/+0xe4) moves by the step. Arrived (distance 0): position := destination; with no target: out of the main list, slot 29 (face) with the current facing; with a target: out of the list and the target's slot 13 is called (RunVerb for a clickable object, NextWaypoint for the walk area) and its 2 or 3 is returned. Not yet arrived: if the step would pass the destination the position snaps to it (arrival next frame), then the position is set (0x401d30 without touching the target position) and the scale updated; returns 1. **Port:** not ported (0x401860, 0x401d30 and the scale function are).
