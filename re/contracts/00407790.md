# 00407790 — inferred_Jim_Update (vtable slot 4)
Status: understood · Cluster: 29 · Convention: fastcall(Jim *this) → int
**Behaviour (read, not ported):** not walking (+0xd5 = 0): the scale (+0xfc) := the walk area's perspective scale at his position (0x414740, 0x100 when there is no walk area), then the speaker update 0x4101f0 (see Speaker). Walking: the vector to the destination (dx, dy); the animation is advanced (0x401860) and the movement its current frame adds since the last frame (fields +0x28/+0x2a of the player against +0xe8/+0xec) is multiplied by the scale (0x100 = 1:1); the object's own tick (slot 2) runs; for left/right facing (3, 4) the vertical step is corrected by (stepX * dy) / dx, for up/down (1, 2) the horizontal step by (stepY * dx) / dy, so he follows the straight line to the destination at the speed of the animation; the 24.8 position (+0xe0/+0xe4) moves by the step. Arrived (distance 0): position := destination; with no target: out of the main list, slot 29 (face) with the current facing; with a target: out of the list and the target's slot 13 is called (RunVerb for a clickable object, NextWaypoint for the walk area) and its 2 or 3 is returned. Not yet arrived: if the step would pass the destination the position snaps to it (arrival next frame), then the position is set (0x401d30 without touching the target position) and the scale updated; returns 1. **Port:** `JimWalker` (core/jimwalker.cpp): gen_vectors_jimwalk.py + drivers/jimwalk_driver.cpp, 1,000 random sessions, 39,146 operations, 52,981 calls identical (the callees are logging hooks; the animation is modelled as the current bank and which banks play); 28 mutations, 27 caught and one equivalent (dx = 0 cannot happen when |dy| < |dx|). Not covered: what the hooked calls do inside, Jim's constructor, his collision test slot 11.

**Fixed finding (5 Nov 2026):** with the Jim vectors regenerated to include the save op (a different random stream), 2 of 400 sessions (30 and 351) differed at a walk update. Cause: after a huge animation step the squared distance to the destination overflows 32 bits; the original's integers wrap and the port's signed arithmetic did not (undefined behaviour, so the compiler's comparison went the other way). The port now sums the squares in unsigned 32-bit arithmetic. 400/400 sessions identical afterwards (19,166 operations).
