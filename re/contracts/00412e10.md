# 00412e10 — inferred_Display_Compose
Status: emu-compared (literal spans only) · Cluster: 32 · Convention: fastcall(Display *this)

**Purpose (confirmed):** Turns the frame's queued spans into pixels. Called by the level's second-pass step (00408800 / Level_Update2) once per frame.
**Behaviour, in order:**
1. If the frame surface is not locked (+0x66a == 0): wait for the DirectDraw back surface (+0xc) to be restorable, Lock it (+0x64 on its vtable) and store its pitch (+0x1c) and memory pointer (+0x678); +0x66a := 1. A lost surface is retried.
2. Sort the deferred scaled sprites (+0x664 entries of 0x20 bytes from +0x24) by depth (selection sort on the u16 at +0xc) and rasterise, through RasterRow (00412120), those whose depth is greater than the display's u16 at +0x668.
3. 00412640: merge the mask queue (+0x674) into the main queue (+0x670).
4. For each of the 480 rows, walk the span list and write pixels to the frame, left to right, **advancing the destination only by span widths** (u16 of x1 − x0 + 1): a span with pixels 1 fills with its colour (+0xe), pixels 0 skips (already drawn), x0 == −1 is a shadow span (each source pixel is looked up through the 32 KB table +0x66c, 5-6-5 or 5-5-5 variants selected by +0x67c; colour +0xe is the table page), anything else copies the pixels. Rows are therefore assumed fully covered (640 pixels, pitch 1280): an empty row dereferences a null pointer. The game always draws a full-screen background first.
5. Draw the queued overlay texts (+0x13a0 entries of 0x70 bytes from +0x680) with 00412c50.
6. Rasterise the remaining deferred sprites (depth ≤ +0x668).
**Port:** ScreenDisplay::compose: steps 2 and 6 for literal scaled images (sorted deepest first; those deeper than the layer depth before the rows, the rest after), and step 4 for copy, solid and none spans. Shadow spans, scaled solid and shadow images, texts and the mask merge are not ported.
**Validation:** Differential check `re/tools/run_startup_check.sh` (gen_vectors_compose.py + drivers/compose_driver.cpp): original reset + AddSprite + Compose (surface pre-locked, no deferred sprites or texts) on 300 cases: a 640x480 background (4 random pixel rows) at depth 65535 plus 1-14 small literal sprites per batch at random places and depths including off-screen edges; after each compose the CRC-32 of each of the 480 frame rows (buffer pre-filled with 0xab to expose misses): 626/626 frames, 300480/300480 rows identical. Mutations caught: span width, pixel source offset, first row, plus the cases below. Solid spans are covered since the type 3 images were added (40% of the images). Scaled literal images are covered by 614 composed frames with up to 40 scaled images each (see 004108a0). Not covered: none/shadow spans, scaled sprites, texts, the lock step, partially covered rows.
**Open questions:** how the lock's pitch relates to 1280 (the flush assumes it); the colour table use of +0x66c and +0x67c in the shadow path.
