# 00414740 — inferred_WalkArea_ScaleAt
Status: understood · thiscall(WalkArea *this, int x, int y) → int scale (0x100 = full size)
**Behaviour (read, not ported):** the perspective scale at a point from the polyline: for each segment (consecutive points of the current sequence, with a scale per point at record +0x14) whose x range contains x, the y of the segment at x is found; the first two such segments (one above and one below the point) are interpolated vertically between their scales (the scale at x on each segment is interpolated along it first); a point not between two segments gets the scale of the single segment found, and 0x100 when none is found. Integer divisions truncate toward zero. **Port:** `WalkArea` (core/walkarea.cpp): gen_vectors_walkarea.py + drivers/walkarea_driver.cpp, 1,200 random polylines, 10,878 operations (scale queries including exact corners, routes with ties, corner stepping) identical; 16 mutations, 12 caught, 4 equivalent (equal scales at a shared corner, equal-distance order on a tie that gives the same corner, an unreachable limit, a step at the end that is handled before). Deviation: a vertical segment hit exactly (the original divides by zero) is skipped.
