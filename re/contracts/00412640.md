# 00412640 — Display_Compose_MergeMask (merge of the mask queue into the main queue)
Status: emu-compared · Cluster: 32 · Convention: fastcall(Display *this)
**Behaviour (read, not ported):** for each of the 480 rows, merges the spans of the mask queue (+0x674, shadow images) into the main queue row (+0x670): a mask span only takes effect where the main row's span is nearer than... it compares depths (the u16 at span +0xc against the mask span's depth range), splits the main spans around it, and marks the covered stretch with x0 = −1 and the shadow table page in +0xe so the flush darkens the pixels already queued (source pointer 0 → points at the frame, 1 → solid colour looked up through +0x66c). With an empty mask queue it does nothing.
**Read in full (5 Nov 2026):** for each row, the shadow spans are taken in list order; each finds the first main span that reaches its start. The shadow misses that span when the shadow has a depth window (+4 as two u16) and the span's depth is outside it, or, without one, when the shadow's depth is greater than the span's. A missing shadow is dropped when it ends inside the span (its node goes to the free list) and otherwise is cut to start after the span. A shadow that hits turns the covered part into a *shadow span*: x0 = -1, x1 = the number of pixels (not a coordinate), colour = the page, pixels = the source: a literal span keeps its pixel pointer (advanced by the pixels cut off the front), a drawn span (pixels 0) points into the frame row at its x0, and a solid span (pixels 1) is darkened at once: colour = table[page * 256 + ((c >> 1) & 0x38e | (c >> 9) & 0x70)] (16-bit entries, 5-6-5). The cases are: same start (shadow longer, equal or shorter than the span: shadowed whole, shadowed whole, or the span keeps its end), a start inside the span that goes past the end (tail shadowed), ends with the span, ends inside it (span, shadow node, rest). A shadow node that is used up is the node the shadow itself came from. Quirk kept: a shadow span's x0 and x1 stop being coordinates, so a second shadow over a shadowed stretch finds nonsense (the original faults or draws garbage); shadows are assumed not to overlap each other on screen.
**Port (5 Nov 2026, `codex/object-kinds`):** `RenderQueue::mergeMask` (core/renderqueue.cpp), called for every row by `ScreenDisplay::compose` before the rows are laid down. Deviation: new nodes come from the main row's pool (the original takes them from the mask row's pool); only a full pool would show the difference. The shade table is `ScreenDisplay::setShadeTable`, loaded from TABLA1.TBL by `World::loadSpeech`; the 5-5-5 variant is not ported (the engine's pixels are 5-6-5). **Validation:** `re/tools/run_startup_check.sh` (gen_vectors_shadow.py + drivers/shadow_driver.cpp): the ORIGINAL AddSprite, scaled literal sprites and Display_Compose (merge and flush) with a random 32 KB table: 456 composed frames (179 with shadows) of 480 rows, every row's CRC identical; 165 more frames with several shadows were checked for the mask queue only (overlapping shadows are undefined in the original). 21 of 24 mutations caught; the three that survive leave only zero-length leftovers or the depth of a node nothing reads. Not covered: scaled shadow images and scaled solid images (the raster routines 00410fb0 and 00411580 are not ported), shadows over shadows.
