# 004123e0 — inferred_Display_FadeOut (was Display_StartEffect)
Status: emu-compared · Cluster: 32 · Convention: thiscall(Display *this, int steps, int delayMs) — RET 8

**Purpose (confirmed):** Fades the last composed frame to black, blocking, in `steps` steps of `delayMs` each. The game calls it with (4, 100) when leaving a menu.
**Behaviour:** copies the frame from the locked surface (+0x678, 640×480×2 bytes) into a temporary buffer (pool arena 3), step = 0x100 / steps, then for factor = 0x100 − step down to 0 in steps of `step` (4 steps: 192, 128, 64, 0): locks the back surface again if it is not locked (retrying while the surface is lost, then GetSurfaceDesc and Lock), builds three 64-entry tables, red[i] = ((i·factor) >> 8) << 11 (<< 10 for 5-5-5), green[i] = ((i·factor) >> 8) << 5, blue[i] = (i·factor) >> 8, rewrites the whole frame from the saved copy as red[p >> 11 & 31] | blue[p & 31] | green[p >> 5 & 63] (5-5-5: shifts 10 and 5, mask 31), unlocks, blits the back surface to the primary one, clears +0x66a, and busy-waits until `delayMs` have passed since the start of the step.
**Edge cases:** steps of 0 divides by zero, more than 256 steps loops forever (step 0); the port warns and returns.
**Port:** ScreenDisplay::fadeOut(steps, delayMs, FadePresenter&): the host shows each frame and keeps the pace (the engine's WindowFade).
**Validation:** Differential check `re/tools/run_startup_check.sh` (gen_vectors_fade.py + drivers/fade_driver.cpp): the original run in Unicorn on a frame filled with a known pattern, 16 cases of 1-8 steps in both pixel formats (the DirectDraw surface calls are fake COM objects, GetTickCount advances): the CRC-32 of the frame after every step, 16/16 cases and 74 frames identical. 5 mutations caught (last step, red shift for 5-5-5, green mask, scaling, step size). Not covered: more than 8 steps (about five million emulated instructions per step), a lost surface.
