# 00409790 — inferred_Polygon_Overlaps (Outline::overlaps)
Status: emu-compared · Cluster: 12 · Convention: cdecl(Outline *a, Outline *b) · returns bool (AL)

**Purpose (confirmed):** whether outline b touches outline a, found by walking chains of both side by side from the left and from the right and watching three flags (b seen above, b seen below, a crossing seen); it answers true as soon as the flags show both sides, or on a crossing. Not symmetric: the caller asks both ways round.
**Behaviour:** four passes: (1) b's first chain against a's first chain, left to right; (2) b's second chain against a's chain going back from where pass 1 ended; (3) b's first chain against a's second chain from the end; (4) b's second chain against a's second chain forwards. Points of equal x use the y order; otherwise the sign of the cross product of the edge and the point decides ("on or below" and "above"). Flags and index moves are exactly those of the port.
**Caller:** 00408c50 (ResolveOverlap), both ways, per step.
**Port:** `Outline::overlaps`. **Validation:** `re/tools/gen_vectors_outline.py`: 3,000 random convex outline pairs (1,067 touching), result identical. Mutations caught: pass-3 start test, flag copy, pass-4 compare. Not covered: non-convex outlines, which the shipped data may contain.
