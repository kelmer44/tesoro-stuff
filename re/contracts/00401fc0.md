# 00401fc0 — inferred_Camera_UpdateToward
Status: emu-compared · Cluster: 4 · Convention: thiscall(Camera *this, int divisor) — RET 4

**Purpose (confirmed):** Per-frame camera step for one room. (1) With a target: if its x is more than 0x4b left of the goal x and the left bound is more than 0x140 left of the goal, the goal moves 100 left and a glide of 0x2ee (750) ms starts from the current centre; then the same to the right (target more than 0x4b right of the goal, goal + 0x13f below the right bound, goal +100). Both tests can fire in one call. (2) If a glide is running: when its time is over the duration, the glide ends, the centre goes to the goal (clamped) and the goal becomes the clamped centre; otherwise the centre is the start plus (goal - start) * elapsed / duration per axis (32-bit multiply, truncating division), clamped. (3) Stores the divisor at +0x3c and the centre scaled by 300 / divisor at +0x34/+0x38 (divisor 0 faults in the original).
**Layout (0x40 bytes, level +0x80, built zeroed by the level):** +0 target object (null for none); +4/+8 start x,y of a glide; +0xc glide start time; +0x10 glide duration (0 = none); +0x14/+0x18 goal x,y; +0x1c min x, +0x20 max y, +0x24 max x, +0x28 min y (bounds for the centre); +0x2c/+0x30 current centre x,y; +0x34/+0x38 centre scaled by 300 / divisor; +0x3c the divisor. Which of the two y bounds is the top of the screen is not established (Level_Load passes -320, 239, 319, -240).
**Callers:** 00408550 Level_UpdateRoomCameras, once per room with that room's dword at +4 as the divisor; 0040ffa0 Obj_DrawSubtitle with the same value of the speaker's room. So the dword at Room +4 (record +0x24) is a camera divisor, and the scaled centre is what the subtitle draw subtracts from the text position (inferred, not checked on screen).

**Port:** Camera::updateToward. **Validation:** Differential check `re/tools/run_startup_check.sh` (gen_vectors_camera.py + drivers/camera_driver.cpp): the original camera functions run in Unicorn (game clock replaced by a settable time) on 600 random sequences of 3-40 operations, including glides to out-of-bounds goals followed by widened bounds and a target scroll (bounds, target on/off, target x including values at the dead-zone edges, direct positions, glides, updates with divisors 1, -7, 100-900, time steps including the exact end of a glide and 32-bit wraparound), after every operation compared the centre position and the scaled position, and the result of SetPositionClamped: 21568/21568 states, 1029/1029 clamp results. Mutations caught: scroll step 99, end of glide `<=`, dead-zone `<=`, goal-x not reset after a glide, right-edge test off by one, left-edge test `<=`, glide start time not stamped, goal y not reset after a glide. Not covered: divisor 0 (crashes in the original; the port warns), the fields that are not observable through the position.
